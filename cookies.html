    How we use cookies and similar tracking technologies on our site.

    Last updated: 15 January 2026

    This Cookies Policy explains how Dovelle Urban Forms (“we”, “us”, or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on www.dovelleurbanforms.com (the “Site”). It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal data more broadly.

    By continuing to browse the Site, you consent to our use of cookies in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, you can adjust your browser settings or refrain from using the Site.

    1. What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site owners.

    Cookies can be “persistent” (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or “session” cookies (which are deleted when you close your browser). They can also be “first-party” (set by us) or “third-party” (set by another domain, such as an analytics provider).

    We also use similar technologies such as web beacons, pixels, and local storage, which function in a comparable way to cookies. For simplicity, this policy refers to all such technologies as “cookies”.

    2. How We Use Cookies

    We use cookies for the following purposes:

    Category Purpose Examples
    Essential Necessary for the Site to function properly. They enable core features such as page navigation, security, and access to protected areas. The Site cannot function without these. Session identifiers, CSRF tokens, load balancer cookies.
    Functional Remember your preferences and choices (e.g., language, region, font size) to provide a more personalised experience. Language preference, dark/light mode setting.
    Analytics Help us understand how visitors interact with the Site by collecting anonymous, aggregated data about page visits, time on page, and referral sources. Google Analytics, Plausible, Fathom.
    Marketing Used to track visitors across websites to display relevant advertisements and measure campaign effectiveness. We currently do not serve ads, but may do so in future. Facebook Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag.

    We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information such as your name, email address, or payment details without your explicit consent.

    3. Specific Cookies We Use

    The table below lists the specific cookies that may be set when you visit the Site. Please note that third-party cookies are controlled by their respective providers and may change over time.

    Name Provider Purpose Duration
    session_id Dovelle Urban Forms Essential — maintains your session state while browsing. Session
    csrf_token Dovelle Urban Forms Essential — protects against cross-site request forgery. Session
    cookie_consent Dovelle Urban Forms Functional — stores your cookie preference choices. 12 months
    _ga Google Analytics Analytics — distinguishes unique users and sessions. 24 months
    _gid Google Analytics Analytics — distinguishes users for reporting purposes. 24 hours
    _gat Google Analytics Analytics — throttles request rate to Google servers. 1 minute
    plausible Plausible Analytics Analytics — anonymised, privacy-friendly visitor tracking. No persistent cookie

    We may update this list from time to time as we add or remove services. Please check back periodically for the latest information.

    4. Third-Party Cookies

    Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies, and they are governed by the privacy policies of the respective third parties. We currently use the following third-party services that may set cookies:

    • Google Analytics — for anonymous usage statistics. Google Privacy Policy.
    • Plausible Analytics — a privacy-focused analytics tool that does not use persistent cookies. Plausible Privacy Policy.

    If we introduce advertising or social media plugins in future, we will update this section accordingly.

    5. Your Cookie Choices

    You have several options for managing cookies:

    • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse or accept cookies, delete existing cookies, and set preferences for specific sites. The “Help” section of your browser will explain how to do this.
    • Opt-out links: For Google Analytics, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
    • Consent tools: If we implement a cookie consent banner, you will be able to manage your preferences directly through the Site.

    Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the Site. Essential cookies cannot be disabled without breaking core features.

    6. Do Not Track Signals

    Some browsers include a “Do Not Track” (DNT) feature that sends a signal to websites indicating you do not wish to be tracked. We currently do not respond to DNT signals because there is no industry consensus on how to interpret them. We will continue to monitor developments in this area and may update our approach in future.

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on this page and updating the “Last updated” date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
