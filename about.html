    • About Dovelle Urban Forms

    An independent editorial publication exploring the spaces we inhabit — and the ideas that shape them.

    We believe that architecture and interior design are not luxuries reserved for the few — they are the silent forces that shape how every one of us lives, works, and rests.

    Dovelle Urban Forms was founded in 2019 with a simple conviction: that thoughtful writing about the built environment should be accessible, rigorous, and useful. Not a glossy catalogue of unattainable perfection, but a practical journal for architects, designers, and anyone who wants to understand the spaces around them more deeply.

    We publish long-form articles, guides, and critical essays on residential architecture, interior design, materials, lighting, space planning, and sustainable building. Our editorial stance is independent — we are not beholden to advertisers or trends. We write about what we believe matters: proportion, light, material honesty, and the quiet intelligence of well-made things.

    Why "Urban Forms"?

    The name reflects our focus on the intersection of the urban and the domestic. Cities are growing denser, housing is becoming smaller, and the way we design our homes has never been more consequential. "Urban Forms" is a nod to the shapes we carve out of the city — the apartments, courtyards, townhouses, and adaptive reuse projects that make modern life possible.

    At the same time, we resist the idea that urban living must mean compromise. Small can be generous. Dense can be green. Minimal can be warm. These are the tensions we explore in every article we publish.

    Who We Write For

    Our readers are architects, interior designers, students, and informed homeowners. They are people who care about how a room feels, not just how it looks. They want to understand why a material works, how a lighting scheme functions, and what makes a floor plan succeed or fail.

    We assume intelligence, not expertise. Whether you are specifying finishes for a new build or simply trying to make your living room feel more peaceful, our goal is to give you knowledge you can use.

    Our Editorial Approach

    Every article we publish is written by a human being with direct experience in the field. We do not use AI to generate content. We do not accept payment for positive coverage. We cite sources, credit photographers, and correct our mistakes openly.

    We are also committed to representing the full diversity of the built environment — not just high-end residential work, but social housing, adaptive reuse, vernacular traditions, and the everyday architecture that surrounds us all.

    About the Editor

    Elena Marchetti is an architect and writer based in Milan. She has contributed to Domus, Dezeen, and Architectural Review.

    How We Work

    1. Pitch & Planning. Every article begins with a clear thesis and an outline. We ask: what will the reader learn? Why does this matter now?
    2. Research & Interviews. We speak to architects, designers, manufacturers, and residents. We visit buildings when possible and consult primary sources.
    3. Writing & Editing. Drafts go through at least two rounds of editing. We prioritize clarity, accuracy, and readability above all else.
    4. Fact-Checking. Claims are verified. Dimensions, material specifications, and historical references are checked against reliable sources.
    5. Publication & Correction. We publish, monitor feedback, and correct errors transparently. Our readers trust us because we earn it.
